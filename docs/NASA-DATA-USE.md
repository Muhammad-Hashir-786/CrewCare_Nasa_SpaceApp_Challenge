# NASA HRP — 5 Hazards

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/

Taken: The five spaceflight hazard categories and the cited mission context: Mars is about 140 million miles away, a Mars trip is about three years, and Mars gravity is 3/8 of Earth's. No health measurements or thresholds were taken.

Changed in CrewCare: The Spaceflight health guide is organized around spaceflight hazards and uses the Mars facts as educational context. The facts do not set the app's check-in or alert thresholds.

# NASA HRP — 17 Risks

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/

Taken: NASA's risk topics, including SANS, decompression sickness, cardiovascular adaptation, hypoxia, and sleep risk. No astronaut records, measurements, or clinical cutoffs were taken.

Changed in CrewCare: The risk and emergency education, eye-scan context, bends topic, sleep tracking context, and related app explanations. These topics do not produce NASA-derived clinical risk scores.

# NASA HRP — Ongoing Research

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/

Taken: Research descriptions used as educational context for Tempus Pro telemedicine, the thigh cuff, B-Complex, actigraphy, OCT eye imaging, and Ultrasound-2. No study results or measurement values were imported.

Changed in CrewCare: The “What NASA is testing now” and imaging education sections. CrewCare does not connect to these devices.

# NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)

Source: https://osdr.nasa.gov/

Taken: No dataset, record, measurement range, or trend value. OSDR was reviewed as a potential research-data source and is not a live vital-signs feed.

Changed in CrewCare: A provenance notice explains that OSDR data are not integrated, that the displayed thresholds are illustrative CrewCare demo rules, and that CrewCare does not pull live NASA telemetry. OSDR did not set the app's measurement ranges or trend ideas.

# Canadian Space Agency — Radi-N2 radiation research

Source: https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/sciences/radi-n2.asp

Taken: The context that neutron radiation is about 30% of total radiation on the ISS and can damage DNA. No dose number or exposure limit was taken.

Changed in CrewCare: The radiation education and emergency context. The information does not set a radiation threshold or dose limit in the app.
